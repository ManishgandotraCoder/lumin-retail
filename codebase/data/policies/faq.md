# Lumen Retail – Frequently Asked Questions

Updated 1 November 2026 · Owner: Customer Support

## Account and orders
**Can I change my delivery address after ordering?**
Yes, until the order is shipped. Contact support with your order number.

**Can I cancel my order?**
Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they are shipped. After shipping, please wait for delivery and start a return.

## Payments
**Which payment methods do you accept?**
UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, and Cash on Delivery (up to ₹10,000).

**Will the assistant ask for my card details?**
No. Lumen will never ask for card numbers, CVV, OTPs or passwords in chat. Never share them.

## Sizes
**How do Lumen footwear sizes work?**
Footwear sizes are UK/India sizes. Velocita running shoes run slightly small; if you are between sizes, choose the larger size.

## Stores
**Where are your stores?**
Mumbai (Lower Parel) and Bengaluru (Indiranagar). Both offer Click & Collect.

## Support
**How do I reach a human?**
Ask the assistant to connect you to support, or email support@lumenretail.example. Support hours are 9 am – 9 pm IST, every day.
