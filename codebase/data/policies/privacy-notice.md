# Lumen Retail – Privacy Notice for the Lumen Concierge Assistant

Version 1.0 · Effective 1 November 2026 · Owner: Data Protection Officer

## 1. What data the assistant uses
- Your name, customer number and the orders linked to your account.
- What you type in the chat, and shopping preferences you share (budget, size, favourite categories).
- The assistant does not need, and will not ask for, card numbers, CVV, OTPs, passwords or government ID numbers.

## 2. Why we use it
- To answer your questions, recommend products, show order status and prepare return requests.
- We do not use chat data to make decisions about credit, employment or anything outside shopping support.

## 3. How long we keep it
- Chat history and saved shopping preferences are kept for **30 days** and then deleted.
- You can delete your chat history at any time with the "Forget my chat" option.

## 4. Who can see it
- Lumen customer support staff, for support and return approvals.
- Our AI service providers process messages only to generate replies, through our AI gateway, which applies safety and privacy controls.
- We do not sell personal data.

## 5. Your rights
- Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, and you can withdraw consent.
- Contact the Data Protection Officer at privacy@lumenretail.example.
