# Lumen Retail – Consumer Rights Summary

Version 1.0 · Effective 1 November 2026 · Owner: Legal & Compliance
This is a plain-language summary for customers. It is not legal advice.

## 1. Your rights as a consumer in India
- The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 gives consumers the right to be protected against unfair trade practices and defective goods, and the right to seek redress.
- The Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 require online sellers to show clear return, refund, exchange, warranty and grievance information.

## 2. Our grievance process
- Grievance Officer: grievance@lumenretail.example
- We acknowledge complaints within **48 hours** and aim to resolve them within **one month** of receipt.

## 3. Pricing and offers
- Prices shown include GST.
- We do not use fake countdown timers, hidden charges or pre-ticked add-ons.

## 4. If you are not satisfied
- You can contact the National Consumer Helpline (1915) or file a complaint on the e-Daakhil consumer commission portal.
