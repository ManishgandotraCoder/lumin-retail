# Lumen Retail – Delivery and Shipping Policy

Version 2.0 · Effective 1 August 2026 · Owner: Logistics

## 1. Delivery options
| Option | Time | Charge |
|---|---|---|
| Standard delivery | 3–6 business days | Free on orders of ₹999 or more, otherwise ₹79 |
| Express delivery | 1–2 business days (metro cities only) | ₹149 |
| Click & Collect | Ready in 4 hours at Mumbai and Bengaluru stores | Free |

## 2. Cash on Delivery (COD)
- Available for orders up to ₹10,000.
- A COD handling fee of ₹49 applies.

## 3. Order tracking
- A tracking reference and courier name are shared by SMS and email once the order ships.
- Customers can ask the Lumen assistant for order status using their order number. The assistant only shows orders that belong to the logged-in customer.

## 4. Delays
- Delivery dates are estimates. Weather, festivals and courier issues can cause delays.
- If an order is more than 3 business days late, customers can contact support for help.

## 5. Failed delivery
- The courier attempts delivery up to 3 times. After that, the order returns to our warehouse and a refund is issued (excluding the COD fee).
