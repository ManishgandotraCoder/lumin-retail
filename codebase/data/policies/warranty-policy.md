# Lumen Retail – Warranty Policy

Version 1.4 · Effective 1 June 2026 · Owner: Product Quality

## 1. Electronics
- All electronics carry the **manufacturer's warranty of 1 year** from the delivery date, unless the product page states otherwise.
- Laptops from Zentro carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty. An optional Lumen Care extended warranty adds 1 more year and must be bought within 30 days of delivery.
- Warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover physical damage, liquid damage or unauthorised repairs.

## 2. Footwear
- Footwear has a **90-day warranty against manufacturing defects** such as sole separation or broken stitching under normal use.
- Normal wear and tear is not covered.

## 3. Home products
- Small appliances (coffee makers, air purifiers): 1-year manufacturer warranty.
- Furniture (chairs): 6-month warranty on mechanisms and frames.

## 4. How to claim
- Contact Lumen support with the order number and a description or photos of the defect.
- For electronics, claims may be handled by the brand's authorised service centre.
- Keep the invoice; it is the proof of purchase for warranty claims.
