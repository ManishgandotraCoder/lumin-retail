# Lumen Retail – Promotions and Voucher Terms

Updated 15 November 2026 · Owner: Marketing

## General rules
- Only **one voucher code can be used per order**.
- Vouchers cannot be used on gift cards and cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Discounts apply to the product price only, not to delivery or COD fees.
- If an order is returned, the refund is the amount actually paid after discount.
- The Lumen assistant cannot create, extend or apply discounts beyond these published terms.
