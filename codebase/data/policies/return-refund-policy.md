# Lumen Retail – Return and Refund Policy

Version 3.2 · Effective 1 October 2026 · Owner: Customer Operations

## 1. Return window
- Footwear, apparel and home products can be returned within **30 days of delivery**.
- Electronics can be returned within **15 days of delivery**.
- The return window is counted from the delivery date shown on the order.

## 2. Condition of returned items
- Footwear and apparel must be unused, unwashed, with original tags and box.
- Home products must be unused and in original packaging.
- Electronics can be returned only if (a) the box is unopened and the seal is intact, or (b) the product is defective or damaged on arrival. Opened electronics that work correctly cannot be returned for change of mind.

## 3. Non-returnable items
- Innerwear, socks and swimwear
- Personalised or engraved products
- Opened personal-care and hygiene products
- Gift cards

## 4. How to start a return
1. Customer requests a return through the Lumen assistant, the website or customer support, quoting the order number.
2. A return request is created with status **Pending approval**.
3. A Lumen support team member reviews every request. The assistant cannot approve returns or refunds.
4. After approval, a free pickup is scheduled within 2–4 business days.

## 5. Refunds
- Refunds are issued only after the returned item passes a quality check at our warehouse.
- Refunds go to the original payment method within **5–7 business days** after the quality check.
- For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are made by bank transfer or UPI.
- Delivery charges are refunded only if the item was defective, damaged or wrong.

## 6. Exchanges
- Size exchanges for footwear and apparel are free within the 30-day window, subject to stock.

## 7. Damaged or wrong items
- Report damaged, defective or wrong items within **48 hours of delivery** with photos, so we can arrange a replacement or refund.
